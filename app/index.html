Sources and method

Day-ahead prices (updated daily), generation by source and demand (updated weekly): ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. Daily averages are weighted by interval length (hourly until September 2025, 15-minute after). Days and months follow Brussels time.

Country price for countries with several zones (Italy, Denmark, Sweden): simple mean of the zones, not the PUN. EU27 price: country prices weighted by monthly demand. Cyprus and Malta have no coupled day-ahead market.

Household prices: Eurostat (nrg_pc_204, nrg_pc_202), half-yearly, all taxes included. Pump prices: European Commission Weekly Oil Bulletin. TTF, storage and the weekly indicators: DG ENER weekly newsletter.
