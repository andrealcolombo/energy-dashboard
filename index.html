Prices through the day

The price changes every hour. Solar pushes it down around midday; when the sun sets, demand is still high and the price rises. Storage charges when power is cheap and sells when it is dear, which flattens the day. These charts show an average day for each year, so the shapes can be compared.

What produced the power, hour by hour · —

Average output for each hour, MW. Below zero: storage charging. Line: demand. Where demand is above generation the country imports, where it is below it exports. ENTSO-E reports batteries only from 2025 and for some countries (not Germany); pumped hydro is reported since 2021.

Daily price swing · highest hour minus lowest hour, % of the day's average

Measured on the average day of each period, so it shows the regular daily pattern, not single extreme days. A smaller swing means a flatter day. Storage, demand response, interconnections and the mix all play a part: these data alone cannot separate their effects.

How to read it: each axis is the country value divided by the value of the comparison (the EU27 average or the country chosen above). The grey shape is the comparison (1×); the rings mark 0.5×, 1.5×, 2× and 2.5×. A point outside the grey shape means the country is above the comparison on that indicator; inside means below. Values above 2.5× are capped (▲). Hover or click a point for the actual values.

Sources and method

Day-ahead prices (updated daily), generation by source and demand (updated weekly): ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. Daily averages are weighted by interval length (hourly until September 2025, 15-minute after). Days and months follow Brussels time.

Country price for countries with several zones (Italy, Denmark, Sweden): simple mean of the zones, not the PUN. EU27 price: country prices weighted by monthly demand. Cyprus and Malta have no coupled day-ahead market.

Household prices: Eurostat (nrg_pc_204, nrg_pc_202), half-yearly, all taxes included. Pump prices: European Commission Weekly Oil Bulletin. TTF day-ahead (weekly average) and EU gas storage: internal elaborations of the European Commission (DG ENER), updated weekly.

Independent prototype built from public data (ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, Eurostat, European Commission Weekly Oil Bulletin) and internal elaborations of the European Commission for TTF and gas storage. Not an official publication of the European Commission.
